🎮 Crazy Plates: Survive the Chaos

🕹️ Controls

  • Move: W A S D or Arrow Keys
  • Jump: Space
  • Rotate Camera: Right Click + Drag
  • Zoom: Mouse Wheel

🎯 Objective

Stay alive for as long as possible on an ever-changing field of plates. Each plate can trigger random events. Your skill and reaction time will be tested to the limit!

⚙️ Features

  • Cross-platform input system (Unity Input System)
  • Third-person camera with zoom and rotation
  • Physics-based player movement with jumping
  • Dynamic random plate events that increase in difficulty
